THE IDEA BEHIND SKY’S COFFEE

A simple cup.
A thoughtful experience.

A coffee shop concept brought to life as a complete web application — from finding a favourite to reviewing an order.

This local portfolio demo connects a product catalogue, customer accounts, a personal cart, and a simulated checkout. The coffee imagery comes from the original SkysCoffee project.

A NOTE ABOUT THIS EXPERIENCE

Built to demonstrate. Designed to explore.

Sample data

Customer accounts and order details are fictional, stored in a local H2 database.

No real transactions

Checkout is a simulation. No payment gateway, email, or delivery service is contacted.

A fresh start

Every application restart restores the original demo accounts and cart.
